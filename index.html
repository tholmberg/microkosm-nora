01 / LOCAL CONTROL

Your machine. Your context.

Nora’s core reasoning environment lives on hardware under the operator’s control. Local information does not need a round trip through somebody else’s cloud just to become useful.

02 / NETWORK BOUNDARY

Egress is a feature, not an assumption.

Network capabilities are intentionally exposed. Connections are selected according to the task instead of granting the assistant indiscriminate access to everything it can reach.

03 / LIVE INTELLIGENCE

Isolation without blindness.

Privacy does not have to mean an air gap. Nora can retrieve live information from approved external systems, bring it across a controlled boundary, and analyze it locally.
